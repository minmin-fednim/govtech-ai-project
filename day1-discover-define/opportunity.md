# Opportunity: Check submissions for errors and missing documents

**Scenario:** B · ICA passport applications

All quotes and metrics are synthetic and labelled as made up.

## Problem Framework

### 1. Goal

Reduce submissions errors or missing documents by half from 17%

### 2. Problem

Applicant did not know what a complete application looked like for their situation

### 3. Context

Applicant submitted passport applications and renewals to ICA

### 4. Constraints

Timelines: 10 weeks

### 5. Success

9 in 10 pass

### 6. Explore

will the applicants edit their submission when they got prompt that there are errors

## Evidence (synthetic)

### User quotes

- The time to renew my passport is longer than my trip! It is unclear what do I need to submit

### Metrics

- error rate,

## Hypothesis

applicants edit their submission when they got prompt that there are errors, and make correct edits

- Risk type: Usability (build a clickable mockup first)
- Kill switch: applicants understand the prompt and able to edit correctly
- Kill-switch signal: applicants stuck or edit and get it wrong
- Success signal: 9 in 10 applications are submitted with complete info and documents by next month
